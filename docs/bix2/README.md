# Bix 2.0 — design candidates

Chunkier, phone-readable Bix. All three keep the orange beanie, round glasses and white face, with a
bigger head, bigger eyes and mouth, a filled body and thick limbs. Same skeleton and pose library as Bix 1.

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `bix2-variants.png` | A, B, C side by side (Bix 1 for scale) |
| `bix2-A-emotions.png` | A — Hoodie: shocked, happy, scared, thinking |
| `bix2-B-emotions.png` | B — Lab coat: shocked, happy, scared, thinking |
| `bix2-C-emotions.png` | C — Chibi: shocked, happy, scared, thinking |
| `bix2-phone-test-180px.png` | every variant × emotion at exactly 180 px tall, light and dark background (view at 100%) |

- **A — Hoodie:** blue hoodie with a small beaker logo, navy trousers, white sneakers with orange soles. Head ≈ 45% of height.
- **B — Lab coat:** open white lab coat over a teal shirt, pens in the pocket, grey trousers, brown shoes. Slightly taller, the most "scientist".
- **C — Chibi:** very big head (≈ 55%), purple sweater with a yellow "?" badge, stubby legs, orange shoes. Most readable at tiny sizes.

Regenerate with `node pipeline/design_sheets.mjs docs/bix2`.
