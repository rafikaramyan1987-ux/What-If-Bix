# Episode plan

From episode 3 the channel runs an **animal-facts season** in the "What if YOU…" format. Each episode has three parts:

1. **The surprising animal fact and WHY it happens** (real biology).
2. **"What if YOU had this ability or body"**, with real science.
3. **A funny Bix-lab ending** (with Newton the cactus) and a teaser for the next episode.

The production rules are the same as episode 2:
- Bix 2.0, a hook in the first seconds, 4–6 min.
- Thumbnail: max 2 big words, Bix with a clear emotion, and the animal.
- 3 Shorts with the hook rules and the SUBSCRIBE ending.
- Piper voice 92 at lengthScale 1.05, SRT captions.
- Metadata with sources and the CC BY 4.0 voice credit.

**Fact rules:**
- Every number or claim needs a reliable source (peer-reviewed paper, university, museum, National Geographic, zoo/aquarium or government science site).
- Flag anything uncertain or debated, and avoid popular myths.
- Each episode gets a `docs/episodeN/FACTS.md` for review before rendering.
