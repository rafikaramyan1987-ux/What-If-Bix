## Deliberately left out

- **"Octopuses have nine brains":** a popular simplification. The arm nerve cords aren't separate brains.
- **"Deoxygenated octopus blood is colorless":** widely repeated, but I didn't find a source I'd cite for the exact appearance, so only "looks blue when it carries oxygen" is narrated.
- **Exact numbers for heart rates and how long the pause lasts:** they vary with species, temperature and activity.
- **"Blue-blooded" royals and methemoglobinemia (people with bluish skin):** off-topic, and easy to get wrong.
